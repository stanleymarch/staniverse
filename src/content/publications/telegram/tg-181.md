С прошлого прошло 9 лет. Такое я точно хотеть на виниле, хоть [Питчфорк оценивает запись всего в 7.3](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/jamie-xx-in-waves/):
Nothing succeeds in dance music like dance music about dancing, and on that front, In Waves is the big time.
You’ll never forget it, unlike a fair bit of In Waves, which is tasteful and slick, approachable and antiperspirant, less oceanic ecstasy than the pool party of the year.
